How a part flows

1 Distribution puts a cylinder body on an empty pallet. 2 Testing checks height and colour. 3 Processing drills and checks the bore. 4 Robot Assembly fits piston, spring and cap. 5 AS/RS stores stock orders or retrieves parts from stock. 6 Handling takes finished or rejected parts off the pallet. 7 Sorting sorts them by colour, rejects to their own chute.
Each pallet carries its order on its ID tag, so each station knows whether to work on it or let it pass.
