How a part flows

A Distribution puts a cylinder body on an empty pallet. C Testing checks height and colour. B Processing drills and checks the bore. D Robot Assembly fits piston, spring and cap. E AS/RS stores stock orders or retrieves parts from stock. F Handling takes finished or rejected parts off the pallet. F Sorting sorts them by colour, rejects to their own chute.
Letters follow the FESTO MPS 500 layout. G is the transfer system. Each pallet carries its order on its ID tag, so each station knows whether to work on it or let it pass.

What the robot does

The cell builds a pneumatic cylinder in five steps: 1 take the drilled body from the pallet into the assembly fixture, 2 insert a piston (black body gets a silver piston, the others a black one), 3 insert the spring, 4 place the cap and screw it a quarter turn, 5 put the finished cylinder back on the pallet.
Each pick waits for its magazine's part-ready signal, so an empty or jammed magazine stops the program at that line.
