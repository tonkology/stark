SMART Twin · in development

The production line, mirrored in software

We are building a digital twin of the labs: a live 3D model students explore before they touch the hardware. You are looking at the idea right now.

  1. Model. Every station in 3D, as it stands on the floor.
  2. Connect. Controller data linked to the model.
  3. Train. Practise on the twin, then run the real line.
